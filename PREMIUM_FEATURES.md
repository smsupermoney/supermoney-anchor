# Supermoney Anchor Platform: Premium Enterprise Features

This document provides a brief overview of the exclusive features available to our Enterprise Anchors. These tools are designed to automate complex workflows, provide deep analytical insights, and streamline financial operations across your entire supply chain.

---

### 1. Comprehensive Dealer Onboarding Workflow

Go beyond simple lead tracking with a complete, state-driven onboarding module. This feature allows you to manage the entire lifecycle of bringing a new dealer onto your platform, managed by a team with specific, granular roles.

**Key Stages & Roles:**
-   **Lead Creation (`sales_person`):** Your sales team can add new dealer leads directly into the system, initiating the onboarding pipeline.
-   **Lead Validation (`sales_manager`):** Sales managers review and approve new leads, ensuring only qualified prospects move forward.
-   **Document Management (`onboarding_ops` & `legal_compliance`):** A central hub to collect, upload, and verify all essential business documents (e.g., GST, PAN). The legal team can perform their due diligence in the same interface.
-   **Site Visits (`field_inspector`):** Dispatch field inspectors who can submit on-site visit reports, complete with notes and image uploads, directly through the platform.
-   **Credit & Limit Approval (`regional_manager`):** Regional managers can perform AI-assisted credit checks and approve the final business limits for the dealer.
-   **Dealer Activation (`dealer_admin`):** The final step where an administrator officially activates the dealer account, making them ready for transactions.

This structured workflow provides complete visibility into your onboarding pipeline and drastically reduces the time it takes to activate a new dealer.

### 2. AI-Powered Risk Assessment

Make smarter, data-driven credit decisions with our AI Risk Assessment tool. Instead of relying solely on historical data, you can generate an on-demand, intelligent risk profile for any dealer in your network.

**How it Works:**
1.  **Select a Dealer:** Choose any dealer from your list.
2.  **Generate Assessment:** With a single click, our AI analyzes the dealer's entire invoice history, payment behavior, overdue patterns, and other key metrics.
3.  **Receive Insights:** The tool generates:
    -   A **Risk Score** (0-100) for a quick, at-a-glance understanding.
    -   A summary of the **Key Risk Factors** that influenced the score.
    -   Actionable **Recommendations** for mitigating the identified risks.

This feature empowers your finance team to proactively manage credit risk and protect your program's financial health.

---

### 3. Streamlined Collections Dashboard

Equip your collections team with a dedicated, powerful interface designed to simplify and accelerate the repayment process.

**Features Include:**
-   **Centralized Tracking:** View all outstanding invoices that require collection in a single, filterable dashboard.
-   **AI-Assisted Repayment Creation:** Simply upload an invoice, and the AI will automatically extract the necessary details (invoice number, amount, due date).
-   **One-Click Link Generation:** Instantly generate and send a unique repayment link directly to the dealer's registered contact information.
-   **Actionable Follow-ups:** Easily resend links, log manual payments, and track the status of each repayment effort, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

This streamlines the collections process, reduces manual effort, and helps improve your cash flow by securing repayments faster.
